Path Slider

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 9, 2025
TL;DR: Path Slider is a fun puzzle game that respects your privacy. We only collect minimal game progress data to provide you with a better experience. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Path Slider! This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you play our game. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Path Slider is developed by RaesNV and is available on the Google Play Store for Android devices.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Game Progress Data

We collect and store the following information locally on your device:

  • Level Progress: Which levels you have completed and unlocked
  • Orb Count: Your in-game currency (orbs) earned from completing levels
  • Game Statistics: Completion times and level attempts

2.2 Google Play Games Services

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games Services, we use Google's services to:

  • Authenticate your identity
  • Display your gamer name or profile name
  • Save your game progress to the cloud
  • Enable progress sync across multiple devices

When you sign in, Google may collect additional information as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we don't collect:

  • ❌ We do NOT collect your email address (unless you sign in with Google)
  • ❌ We do NOT access your contacts, photos, or other personal files
  • ❌ We do NOT track your location
  • ❌ We do NOT use cookies or tracking pixels
  • ❌ We do NOT collect any personally identifiable information beyond what Google Play Games provides

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Save your game progress locally and to the cloud
  • Unlock new levels as you complete previous ones
  • Track your orb currency for in-game progression
  • Sync your progress across devices when signed in with Google Play Games
  • Provide you with a seamless gaming experience

We never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

Game progress is stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. This data remains on your device and is only accessible by the Path Slider app.

4.2 Cloud Storage

If you sign in with Google Play Games, your progress is saved to Google's secure servers. This data is protected by Google's security measures and is only accessible when you're signed in with your Google account.

4.3 Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We use industry-standard practices to safeguard your information.

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Google Play Games Services

Path Slider uses Google Play Games Services for authentication and cloud save functionality. By using these features, you agree to Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Terms of Service
  • Google Play Terms of Service

5.2 Google Play Store

The app is distributed through the Google Play Store, which may collect information about downloads and usage as described in Google's policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Path Slider is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

Parents and guardians should be aware that Google Play Games Services has its own age restrictions and parental controls.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: You can view your game progress anytime within the app
  • Deletion: You can delete your local progress by clearing the app data in Android Settings
  • Sign Out: You can sign out of Google Play Games at any time from the main menu
  • Data Portability: Your progress is automatically synced when signed in with Google Play Games

How to Delete Your Data

To delete all game data:

  • Go to Android Settings → Apps → Path Slider → Storage → Clear Data
  • Or uninstall the app to remove all local data
  • To remove cloud data, sign out of Google Play Games and delete the saved games in the Play Games app

8. International Users

Path Slider is available globally. If you use the app from outside your country of residence, your data may be transferred to and processed in other countries where Google's servers are located. By using the app, you consent to such transfers.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, regulatory, or operational reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Showing an in-app notification (for significant changes)

Your continued use of Path Slider after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated privacy policy.
