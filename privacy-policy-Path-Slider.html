Path Slider

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026
At a glance: Path Slider stores game progress on your device. The free game uses Google AdMob advertising, and optional purchases use Google Play Billing. After the required consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland — or after the consent flow finishes elsewhere — Google Firebase may process analytics, crash and performance data. We do not run an account system or collect your progress on our own servers.

1. Overview

Path Slider is an offline puzzle game developed by RaesNV. The game does not require an account, and we do not operate servers that receive your game progress or other personal data. Your game progress is stored on your device. The free version uses Google AdMob to show advertisements, optional purchases are handled by Google Play, and Google Firebase may process analytics, crash and performance data as described below.

Google may process personal data through these services, as explained in this policy.

2. Data stored on your device

Path Slider stores the following data locally:

  • Completed levels, stars, best times, statistics, achievements, streaks and hint credits
  • Settings such as sound, music, vibration and theme
  • A locally cached indication of your “Remove Ads” purchase status
  • Generated puzzle cache

We do not receive this data. Depending on your Android backup settings, some app data may be included in a Google account backup or transferred to another device. Clearing app data or uninstalling normally removes local data, but copies in backups may remain subject to Google's backup settings and retention.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

Unless you have purchased “Remove Ads”, Path Slider may display banner ads, interstitial ads and optional rewarded video ads supplied by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited and/or Google LLC).

To deliver, measure and secure advertisements, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process:

  • Device identifiers, potentially including your Android advertising ID
  • IP address, which may indicate an approximate location
  • Device and application details, such as device model, operating system, language and app version
  • Ad impressions, clicks, interactions and diagnostic information

Consent and privacy choices

For users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app is designed to request advertising consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) / IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF) before requesting ads where required. You may accept, reject or manage available consent choices. Declining personalised advertising does not prevent you from playing. Limited or non-personalised ads may still appear where legally permitted.

You can revisit your options using Settings → Ad privacy choices (where available in the installed version). Android also provides advertising ID controls in its privacy settings; the exact menu varies by device.

Rewarded video ads are optional and may provide an in-game reward such as a hint or extra time.

For more details, see How Google uses information from apps using its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics, crash and performance — Google Firebase

If you allow analytics in the consent choices — or if you are outside a region where the consent form is required — the app may send limited event, crash and performance data to Google Firebase / Google Analytics (Google Ireland Limited and/or Google LLC). This helps us understand how people play, whether the app is stable, and how it performs. It is not used to create a Path Slider account.

The following Google Firebase products may be used:

  • Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics for Firebase — gameplay and usage events
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports and related diagnostic data
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring — app start and performance traces

Firebase may process:

  • An app instance identifier (reset when you uninstall the app or when collection is disabled)
  • Gameplay events such as level start/end, tutorial progress, screen views and ad-load results (not puzzle solutions)
  • Coarse device data Google collects automatically: country derived from IP address, language, device model, operating system and app version
  • Crash stacks (Firebase Crashlytics) and performance traces (Firebase Performance Monitoring)
  • The Android advertising ID only when ad-related consent also allows it

We do not send your name, email address, precise GPS location, purchase tokens, payment details or local puzzle solutions to Firebase.

Consent in the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland

Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring are off by default. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the legal basis is consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), collected through the same Google UMP / TCF form used for advertising. Advertising consent is not treated as analytics consent. Collection stays off until you grant the measurement purposes (TCF Purpose 1 together with Purpose 8 or 10).

Outside those regions, this processing may start after the UMP flow finishes, without a separate analytics form.

You can withdraw consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset your advertising ID in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

More information: Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

5. In-app purchases — Google Play Billing

Path Slider offers an optional one-time “Remove Ads” purchase, processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details. The app receives purchase status and a purchase token from Google Play to verify and restore purchases. Google's own handling of payment information is described in its Google Play Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

6. Sharing and third parties

The “Share result” feature opens the Android share sheet with game-related text. No result is shared unless you choose a destination and complete the sharing action.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not send your local game progress to our own servers. We share data with the following Google service providers, as described in this policy:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google Play Billing — the optional “Remove Ads” purchase
  • Google Firebase / Google Analytics — analytics, crash reporting and performance monitoring, when analytics consent is granted or when a consent form is not required

Any app you choose through Android's share sheet has its own privacy practices.

7. Legal bases under the GDPR

  • Local gameplay data: processing necessary to provide the game you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), where personal data processing is involved.
  • Personalised advertising and consent-based device access: consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and applicable ePrivacy rules), which can be withdrawn.
  • Analytics, crash reporting and performance monitoring: consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Outside those regions, this processing may start after the UMP flow finishes, without a separate analytics form.
  • Purchases: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Google may act as an independent controller for certain processing and describes its own legal bases in its privacy documentation. Other advertising or analytics processing, where applicable, depends on the purposes and choices disclosed in the consent interface.

8. Children

Path Slider is not specifically directed to children under 13 or the applicable age of digital consent in their country. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. If you have concerns about a child's information, please contact us. Age-related obligations and ad settings must be configured consistently with the app's actual target audience in Google Play and AdMob.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict or port personal data, object to certain processing, withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Because we do not maintain a user database, you can generally remove locally stored game data via Android Settings → Apps → Path Slider → Storage → Clear data or by uninstalling the app. Backup copies are managed through your device and Google account settings.

For data processed by Google AdMob, Google Play or Google Firebase / Google Analytics, consult the Google Privacy Policy, Google Account and Firebase privacy information. You can withdraw in-app consent via Settings → Ad privacy choices.

If you are in Belgium, you can also contact the Belgian Data Protection Authority.

10. Security and international processing

Local game data is kept in Android app storage, subject to Android's platform security protections. Google SDKs communicate over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No technical safeguard can guarantee absolute security.

Google services may process data outside your country. Please consult Google's privacy information for details of international transfers and applicable safeguards.

11. Retention and deletion

  • Local game data: generally remains until you clear the app data or uninstall the game, subject to device backups.
  • Google AdMob and Google Play: we do not set the retention periods for data independently processed by those services; consult Google's privacy documentation and available controls.
  • Google Firebase / Google Analytics: retention follows the retention setting of the Google Analytics property. Crashlytics crash reports and Performance Monitoring traces are retained according to Google's product settings. App instance identifiers are reset when you uninstall the app or when collection is disabled.

To delete local game data, use Android Settings → Apps → Path Slider → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. Requests about Google's processing should be made through Google's tools linked above.
